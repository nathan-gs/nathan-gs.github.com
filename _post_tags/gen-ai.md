---
title: Generative AI
---

Generative AI covers the models that produce text, code, images, and audio — large language models and the applications built directly on them. For most people it arrives as a chat window: fast, capable, and personal.

Gen AI is often considered *Personal AI* in comparison to [Organisational AI](https://scrydon.com/platform/ai-os/organisational-ai/). A copilot is brilliant for the person using it, but it has no shared memory of the business — every session starts empty, and nothing it works out accrues anywhere. Getting from the first to the second is what an [AI OS](/tags/ai-os) is for.
